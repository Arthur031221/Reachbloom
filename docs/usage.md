# Using the map

Choose BART rail, Maywood Express Shuttle or River Otter Ferry from Example network. Each starts on a date with service in the bundled snapshot. The vehicle dots show scheduled positions, not live GPS.

1. Set the service date and departure time in the agency timezone.
2. Drag the orange pin or click the map to place A.
3. Choose a travel budget from 15 to 90 minutes and one to eight rides.
4. Filter modes, then search a stop by name or ID.
5. Click a stop to read its timed walking, waiting and ride itinerary. This search has a 90 minute horizon.
6. Enable B to compare another origin and departure. Use the same origin button to compare two departure times at one location.
7. Share a bundled example or export a 1080 x 1350 PNG.

Orange is A, teal is B and purple is the common reachable area. This is straight line walking, including across roads, barriers and water. It is a meeting area comparison, not a search for homes near two workplaces.

Play advances the departure clock. The speed choices advance it by 15, 60 or 300 simulated minutes per real second. Crossing midnight advances the date. Positions follow shapes where available, otherwise straight segments between scheduled stops.

Switch language or theme in the panel. Imported ZIPs remain in browser memory, and sharing is disabled for them. A page reload needs the file again. Feed notes show skipped or approximated input. See [accuracy](accuracy.md) before relying on a result.
