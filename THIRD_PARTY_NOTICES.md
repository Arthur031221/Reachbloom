## Bay Area Rapid Transit GTFS

The bundled timetable at `public/data/bart.zip` comes from [BART's official GTFS download](https://www.bart.gov/dev/schedules/google_transit.zip). Redistribution is subject to the [BART developer license agreement](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/developers/developer-license-agreement). Its feed attribution is Bay Area Rapid Transit. This data license is separate from the MIT source license.

## OpenStreetMap

The interactive map requests raster tiles from `tile.openstreetmap.org` and displays OpenStreetMap attribution. Requests follow the browser cache and send the usual browser referrer. There is no bulk tile download or offline tile pack. OpenStreetMap data is available under the [Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The tiles are not bundled with this project.
